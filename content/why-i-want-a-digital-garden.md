# Why I Want a Digital Garden

I want a digital garden because I keep having interesting thoughts from chess, coding, AI tools, games, and life, but they disappear.

I do not want this to become another productivity guilt thing.

I just want a place where my thoughts can slowly become useful.

Maybe this starts as one small note, and that is enough.
